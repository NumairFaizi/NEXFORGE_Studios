Privacy Policy

Information about privacy and data practices in the Bus Jam mobile game.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

NEXFORGE STUDIOS ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Bus Jam ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the App, including through third-party advertising services.

2. Game data stored on your device

The App may store game progress, preferences, levels, rewards, and settings locally on your device to support gameplay. Locally stored information may be removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it, depending on your device and backup settings.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads, which may include banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other technical information to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable settings and law.

Google's practices are described in Google Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance for app developers.

The App does not need to request precise device location permission solely to display AdMob ads; this does not mean advertising services never infer general location from network information.

4. How information may be used

  • To operate and maintain game features and settings.
  • To display and measure advertising, including rewarded ads.
  • To prevent fraud, protect security, and diagnose technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations and applicable privacy choices.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law, the App should request consent or offer privacy choices for advertising technologies. You may also manage certain advertising preferences in your device settings. Availability of consent and privacy controls depends on your region, device, and the App's implemented consent flow.

6. Children's privacy

Bus Jam's intended age audience and its advertising configuration must be reflected accurately in the App's Google Play target-audience declarations. If the App is directed to children or includes children in its target audience, additional child-privacy requirements and advertising restrictions may apply. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns can contact us using the details below.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data can generally be deleted by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App, subject to device backups. Third-party advertising providers may retain information under their own policies and applicable legal requirements. For questions about data associated with the App, contact us below.

8. Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information, but no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised version here with an updated date.
