Flow Garden – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/11/2026

Flow Garden ("the app") is a puzzle game by NEXFORGE Studios. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or exact location, and we do not run our own servers that store personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, undos, themes and settings) is saved only on your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or use Reset Progress.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
