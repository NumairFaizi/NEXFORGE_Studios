NEXBLOCK Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

NEXBLOCK is an offline-capable puzzle game developed by NEXFORGE STUDIOS.

Game data

Your scores, progress, coins, power-ups and preferences are stored on your device. NEXBLOCK does not require an account or collect your name, contacts, precise location or payment details through the game. Android device backup and restore may retain local game data according to your device settings.

Advertising

NEXBLOCK uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may collect or share IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, interactions with ads and products, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and improve ads and prevent fraud and abuse. IP addresses may be used to infer approximate location. Advertising requires an internet connection. Core gameplay can be played without internet.

Privacy choices

Where required, the game presents a consent form and an Ad privacy options entry in Settings. You can manage advertising identifiers and ad personalization using your Android and Google account settings. Learn about Google's data handling at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game, subject to any Android backups. Google processes advertising information under its own retention and privacy practices. Contact us with privacy questions or requests.
